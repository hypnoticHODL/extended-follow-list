Extended follow list

Unofficial tool. It publishes a public kind 30000 list to your relays — anyone can read who is on it, and it reveals your guesses about your social graph. This page never sees your key: signing happens in your browser signer (Alby, nos2x…), and relay queries go straight from your browser to the relays. Not affiliated with Amethyst or Wisp.

FAQ — read this before publishing


Why two (or more) lists?


A relay will not store an event over ~64 KB, and each p tag costs ~72 bytes,
so one list tops out near 700–750 members. The tool splits members by rank into
d=extended, d=extended-2, … Every chunk fits, so every relay
accepts it, and the copies stay consistent.



How do I get one feed out of several lists?


In Amethyst use the All User Follows filter: it unions your contact list with every
people list you own, deduplicated. Selecting a single list shows only that chunk.



I published but nothing changed


Lists are read at startup — force-stop the app and reopen. Also check the publish log:
any relay answering OK false did not store the list.



Someone I expect is missing


Likely one of: they are below threshold (counted against the follow lists actually
fetched); they were cut by max members; you already follow them (candidates exclude
first-degree follows); they are muted (kind 10000); or a relay is serving an older, shorter
copy of the list. The audit box prints the total member count across all chunks, membership and
the newest timestamp per relay.



Relays disagree on the member count


That means a publish was too large for some relays and only partly accepted. Keep
chunk size at 700 or lower and republish until every relay reports the same count and
timestamp.



I shrank the list but old people remain


Old chunks (d=extended-3, …) are still published. Keep retire old
chunks checked and republish: surplus chunk addresses are overwritten with an empty list.
A different d base is a separate family of lists and must be deleted manually. Then
force-stop the app and reopen.



Can members be private?


Not here. Clients that offer "private members" encrypt them into the event content; this
tool publishes public p tags only.



Do I have to review the candidates?


No, but blind publishing keeps the predictable noise: accounts everyone follows, bought
followers, and dead keys with no profile. A useful cheap filter is a higher threshold.
